For gifts by Venmo, Zelle, check or cash. Fill in the form, then save the letter as a PDF or email it. Gifts through the Zeffy form already get a receipt from Zeffy.

Sign in with the same GitHub account you use for the editor.

The gift

This letter says nothing was given in return for the gift. If the donor got something for their money, such as a raffle ticket, a meal or a shirt, it needs different wording: check with the treasurer first.

The letter

Save as PDF or print opens the print window: choose "Save as PDF" there. Email it opens your mail app with the letter as the message. To attach the PDF as well, save it first and drag it into the email.

Nothing typed here is saved or sent anywhere by this page. Keep a copy of each letter for the club's records.
